Entry for the make.fun competition **Five Seconds to Explain the Glass**: the one sentence a house cat would say in its own defence after knocking a glass off the table, at most 200 characters, in the cat's own voice — no apology, no confession.

`main.py` writes that sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) with no trailing newline, and prints it to stdout.
